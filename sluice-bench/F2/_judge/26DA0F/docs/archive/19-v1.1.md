## 2. Label hierarchy (decided 2026-10-13, Q1; built by L1, waiting on HG-L1)

**The problem.** Once every label is a holo plate, a solar region at rest shows roughly 4 homeworlds,
3 stations, 6 captains, the star and up to 16 pieces, all in one cyan. With equal weight, that reads as
chaotic. The user's direction: the most important information (dreadnoughts, captains) at full
contrast and saturation; lower-value information (a planet's title) lower contrast and desaturated.

**Constraints that shape it.**
- **Text stays at AA or better** (13 §1 pillar; [15 §6](../15-reference.md#6-labels-and-type), *Contrast
  rules*: vital text 4.5:1 without exception). So text can be quieter, but never below about
  6:1, to leave margin over the brightest nebula. Most of the quieting comes from the frame, the
  pointer, the glow and the saturation, not from the text.
- **The glass stays one opacity** (`--holo-glass`, 0.86) at every tier, so a label's contrast never
  depends on the sky behind it.
- **One hue family.** Every plate is the HUD cyan family, from saturated to grey-cyan. No ladder hue
  (gold means accomplished), no role hue (opportunity blue, deadly red) and no `--amber` on a plate.
  The art's outlines already carry role and state. The one exception is unchanged: an
  expanded list's rung marks (○ ◔ ◕ ● ⊘) keep the ladder's colours, small and paired with a shape
  (INV-20).
- **Visual weight is separate from placement priority.** `rank` (15 §4: seat 1, facility 2,
  piece 3, agent 4) decides who places first when labels collide. Tiers decide how loud a placed
  label is. A homeworld can place first and still be quiet.

**Three tiers, by what the label names.**

| Tier | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **T1, Lead** | Captains (people), dreadnoughts (repos and their PRs) | The actors: who did the work and where it shipped. Few per scene, and the story follows them |
| **T2, Door** | Galaxies (All projects), pod stars (the round), the frontier's pieces | What you can enter or act on next, at levels where nothing else competes. At 188 real projects, T1 galaxies would shout |
| **T3, Place** | Homeworlds, the region's star (the system you're inside) | Context: where things are. The captain already names the person, and the breadcrumb names the system |

**States lift a label, whatever its tier.** Pointed, selected or expanded → drawn as T1, with the lit
frame and the stronger glow. The selected edge stays ivory (09). The followed ship stays T1 and never
opens (as today). In playback the tiers keep their contrast and only the size steps down (Title
only, then the captain's picture alone).

**Pieces (asteroids, hive ships)** have no label at rest under L7: their plate appears on hover, so
it's always lifted. Their rows in a homeworld's expanded list use the T2 text colours.

**Proposed values** (ratios on the glass over `--ink`, measured 2026-10-13):

| | T1 Lead | T2 Door | T3 Place | Lifted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Title colour | `#D4F7FF` (16.5:1, `--holo-text` today) | `#B7D3DA` (11.9:1) | `#9DB4BA` (8.6:1) | `#D4F7FF` |
| Title type | Inter 12 px, 600 | Inter 12 px, 400 | Inter 11.5 px, 400 | The tier's size, 600 |
| Subtitle colour | `#8FB6C2` (8.6:1, today's) | `#8AA2A9` (7.0:1) | `#8A9EA3` (6.7:1; built 2026-10-13, was `#7E979E`, 6.1:1, which fell to 4.1:1 in the specs' worst case, the glass over white) | `#8FB6C2` |
| Frame and pointer at rest | `--holo-line` at 0.85 | `--holo-line` at 0.5 | a desaturated grey-cyan (about `#8DB3BD`) at 0.4 | `--holo-line` at 1 |
| Rim dot | full | 0.7 | 0.5 | full |
| Glow (text, then frame) | 6 px, 2.5 px | none, 1.5 px | none | 6 px, 4 px |

This also settles a known rough edge: the holo title's weight 500 (90-later, *Code found by the doc
overhaul*), which the browser synthesises because only Inter 400 and 600 are bundled.

**How it would be reviewed.** A new gallery cell, *Label hierarchy*: one plate of each tier at rest,
lifted and in play, over ink and over the brightest nebula; then the Harbor Pod region in the drive at
rest and playing. Guards: `solar.spec.ts` contrast (extended to the galaxy and the round),
`slice1.spec.ts` VC-2, and a new `holo-tiers` table in 15 §6 for `docTables.test.ts`.

**Decided** (§4 Q1, 2026-10-13): these three tiers, their membership and the "states lift" rule are
the direction. L1 built them ([slice-1.1-L1.md](progress/slice-1.1-L1.md)); HG-L1 reviews them.
