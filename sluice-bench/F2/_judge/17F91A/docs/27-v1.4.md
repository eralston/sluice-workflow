## Goal

v1.4 finishes v1.3's leftovers and makes playback **a story more than a history**. Pulsar Pod (20 captains)
plays within a target time set from measured numbers, while Comet Pod (solo) keeps its full drama. Busy work
is told in fewer, larger moments that still name every cause (R13) and make every participant a hero.
Formations form up on screen and clear of their beams, with labels that don't collide. The chart rests
quieter: only what you point at, what you select and what playback emphasizes gets a full plate. v1.4 is
done when every item below is accepted, the human gates are signed, the frame budget (p95 ≤ 2 vsyncs) holds
on Pulsar under the profiles that bake the aura (`phone`, `tablet`; C3), with `desktop` and `laptop-battery`
reported soft with numbers at the final check (amended 2026-11-06), and 90-later holds only what was deferred
here.
